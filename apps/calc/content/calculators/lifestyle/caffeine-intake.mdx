## Caffeine Guidelines

The FDA and most health bodies recommend no more than **400 mg/day** for healthy adults. The calculator uses 5.7 mg/kg as a weight-based cap, but never exceeds 400 mg.

## Caffeine and Performance

Caffeine is one of the most studied ergogenic aids. At the right dose it improves endurance, strength output, reaction time, and focus. It works by blocking adenosine receptors in the brain, reducing perceived effort and fatigue.
