faqs:
  - question: "What is the cholesterol ratio?"
    answer: "The cholesterol ratio divides total cholesterol by HDL (good) cholesterol. A lower ratio is better. Ratios below 3.5 are optimal; above 5 indicate elevated risk."
  - question: "What is HDL cholesterol?"
    answer: "HDL (high-density lipoprotein) is known as 'good' cholesterol because it helps remove other forms of cholesterol from the bloodstream. Higher levels are protective."
  - question: "What is LDL cholesterol?"
    answer: "LDL (low-density lipoprotein) is 'bad' cholesterol that can build up in artery walls. High LDL is a major risk factor for heart disease and stroke."
  - question: "What are normal cholesterol levels?"
    answer: "Total cholesterol below 200 mg/dL is desirable. HDL above 60 mg/dL is protective. LDL below 100 mg/dL is optimal."
