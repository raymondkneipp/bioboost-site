  - question: "What is systolic vs diastolic pressure?"
    answer: "Systolic pressure (the top number) measures the force when your heart beats. Diastolic pressure (the bottom number) measures the force between beats when your heart is at rest."
  - question: "What is normal blood pressure?"
    answer: "Normal blood pressure is below 120/80 mmHg. Elevated is 120–129 systolic with diastolic below 80. High Stage 1 starts at 130/80 or higher."
  - question: "What causes high blood pressure?"
    answer: "Risk factors include excess weight, lack of exercise, high sodium diet, alcohol, stress, smoking, genetics, and age. Most cases have no single cause."
  - question: "Should I see a doctor about my reading?"
    answer: "Yes, if you consistently see readings at Stage 1 or higher. A hypertensive crisis (above 180/120) requires immediate medical attention."

## Taking an Accurate Reading

- Sit quietly for 5 minutes before measuring.
- Rest your arm at heart level.
- Don't smoke, exercise, or drink caffeine within 30 minutes.
- Take two to three readings and average them.
