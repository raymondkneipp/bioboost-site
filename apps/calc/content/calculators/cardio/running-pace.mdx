## Pace vs Speed

**Pace** measures how long it takes to cover one unit of distance (e.g., 5:00/km).

**Speed** measures how much distance you cover per unit of time (e.g., 12 km/h).

They are reciprocals of each other:

**Speed (km/h) = 60 ÷ pace (min/km)**

**Pace (min/km) = 60 ÷ speed (km/h)**
