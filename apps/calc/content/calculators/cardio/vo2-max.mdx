# VO₂ Max Calculator (Cooper Test)

VO₂ max is the gold standard measure of aerobic fitness. This calculator uses the **Cooper 12-minute run test**, one of the most validated field tests for estimating VO₂ max.

## Cooper Formula

Developed by Dr. Kenneth Cooper in 1968, the formula is:

**VO₂ max = (distance in meters − 504.9) ÷ 44.73**

## Tips for the Test

- Perform the test on a flat surface (track preferred).
- Warm up for 5–10 minutes before starting.
- Maintain a steady, sustainable pace — don't sprint at the start.
- Track distance with a GPS watch, running app, or marked track.
