  - question: "What is a MET value?"
    answer: "MET (metabolic equivalent of task) is a ratio of the rate of energy expenditure during an activity relative to resting. Walking at 3 mph has a MET of ~3.5, meaning it burns 3.5× more energy than sitting."
  - question: "How accurate are calorie estimates?"
    answer: "MET-based estimates have a margin of error of 10–20%. Individual factors like fitness level, efficiency, and body composition affect actual calorie burn. Wearables with heart rate tend to be more accurate."
  - question: "Does fitness level affect calories burned?"
    answer: "Yes. Fitter individuals are more efficient at movement, so they burn fewer calories for the same activity. However, they can sustain higher intensities, which compensates."
  - question: "Do I burn more calories at higher body weight?"
    answer: "Yes — the formula is directly proportional to body weight. A heavier person burns more calories doing the same activity for the same duration."

# Calories Burned Calculator

Select an activity, enter your weight and duration to estimate calories burned using research-validated MET values.

<CaloriesBurnedCalculator />

## How It Works

**Calories = MET × weight (kg) × duration (hours)**

MET values come from the Compendium of Physical Activities, a research database developed at Arizona State University.
