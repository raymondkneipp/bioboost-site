## The Science

The calculation uses the widely accepted figure of **7,700 kcal per kg of body fat**:

**Time (weeks) = (weight to lose in kg × 7,700) ÷ weekly deficit (kcal)**

This assumes the deficit is consistent and applies entirely to fat loss — in practice, some weight loss will be from water and muscle.
