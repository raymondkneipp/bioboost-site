# Body Fat Percentage Calculator

Body fat percentage tells you how much of your total body weight is fat. Unlike BMI, it directly measures body composition and is a better predictor of metabolic health and fitness.

## US Navy Circumference Method

This calculator uses the US Navy formula, which estimates body fat from body circumference measurements.

**Men:**

Body Fat % = 86.010 × log₁₀(waist − neck) − 70.041 × log₁₀(height) + 36.76

**Women:**

Body Fat % = 163.205 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) − 97.684 × log₁₀(height) − 78.387

All measurements in centimeters.

## Measuring Tips

- Use a flexible measuring tape, pulled snug but not tight.
- Take measurements in the morning, before eating.
- Measure three times and average the results.
- Keep the tape horizontal to the floor for all circumferences.
