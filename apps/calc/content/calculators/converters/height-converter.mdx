## Conversion Formula

**Centimeters to feet/inches:**

1. Divide cm by 2.54 to get total inches
2. Divide by 12 for feet; the remainder is inches

**Feet/inches to centimeters:**

(feet × 12 + inches) × 2.54 = cm
