    answer: "A calorie deficit means consuming fewer calories than your body burns. This forces the body to use stored energy (primarily fat) for fuel."
  - question: "How much deficit is safe?"
    answer: "A deficit of 250–500 kcal/day is generally safe and sustainable, producing 0.25–0.5 kg of fat loss per week. More aggressive deficits risk muscle loss and nutrient deficiencies."
  - question: "Is it safe to eat below my BMR?"

## Combining Deficit with Training

A calorie deficit tells your body to use fat for fuel. Resistance training during a deficit helps preserve muscle mass, which is critical for maintaining metabolic rate and achieving a lean physique rather than just losing weight.
