# Maintenance Calories Calculator

Maintenance calories equals your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) — the calories you burn each day. Eat at this level and your weight stays the same.

## Why Maintenance Calories Matter

Before cutting or bulking, knowing your maintenance level is essential:

- **Dieting:** Know how much to subtract for a safe deficit
- **Bulking:** Know how much to add for lean muscle gain
- **Recomping:** Eat at maintenance while training hard to slowly shift body composition

## Tips for Finding Your Real Maintenance

The calculator gives a formula-based estimate, but everyone's metabolism is different. The most accurate approach:

1. Weigh yourself daily for 1 week and average the readings.
2. Track everything you eat in a food diary.
3. Calculate your daily calorie average.
4. If your weight is stable, that's your maintenance.

Adjust by 100 kcal at a time and reassess every 2 weeks.
