  - question: "What macro split is best for fat loss?"
    answer: "Higher protein (40%) helps preserve muscle and increases satiety. Moderate carbs (30%) fuel training. Fat (30%) supports hormones. Adjust based on personal preference and adherence."
  - question: "What is a ketogenic diet?"
    answer: "A ketogenic diet is very low in carbs (5%) and high in fat (70%), forcing the body into ketosis — a metabolic state where fat is the primary fuel source."
  - question: "Do macros matter more than total calories?"
    answer: "Total calories determine weight change. Macros determine body composition and how the weight changes affect muscle vs. fat. Both matter for optimal results."
