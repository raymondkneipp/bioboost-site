faqs:
  - question: "How much protein do I need per day?"
    answer: "The RDA is 0.8 g/kg for sedentary adults, but research supports 1.2–2.2 g/kg for active individuals and those seeking muscle gain or fat loss."
  - question: "Should I eat more protein when cutting?"
    answer: "Yes — higher protein intake (1.8–2.4 g/kg) during a calorie deficit helps preserve lean mass and increases satiety, making fat loss more sustainable."
  - question: "Can I eat too much protein?"
    answer: "Excess protein is converted to glucose or fat and excreted. For healthy adults, up to 2.5 g/kg is considered safe. Those with kidney disease should consult a doctor."
  - question: "Does protein timing matter?"
    answer: "Spreading protein across 3–5 meals maximizes muscle protein synthesis. Post-workout protein (within 2 hours) is beneficial but the daily total matters most."
